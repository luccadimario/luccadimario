### Carmen Lucca DiMario

CS master's student at Embry-Riddle (2027). I build across the stack, from
embedded C and compiler toolchains up through reinforcement learning research
and the applications on top. Two peer-reviewed publications, and a shipped iOS
app at [pfactor.app](https://pfactor.app). More at [dimario.dev](https://dimario.dev).

**Some things I've built**

- **[dilithiumcoin](https://github.com/luccadimario/dilithiumcoin)** is a
  quantum-safe peer-to-peer cryptocurrency written from scratch in Go: node,
  wallet CLI, and CPU/GPU miner, with CRYSTALS-Dilithium (NIST FIPS 204)
  signatures on every transaction.
- **[SEMBAS](https://github.com/luccadimario/SEMBAS)** is my graduate research,
  extending an adaptive boundary scanner to reinforcement learning to
  characterize where a trained policy stays reliable. Ten policies, PPO and SAC,
  1.7M+ training steps.
- **[SiT_vega](https://github.com/luccadimario/SiT_vega)** does LiDAR-based
  pedestrian trajectory prediction with XGBoost and a Social Transformer,
  benchmarked on an HPC cluster.
- **[AirHoundMiddleware](https://github.com/luccadimario/AirHoundMiddleware)** is
  the ROS2 vision-to-flight control link for a UAV tracking system, published in
  *Beyond: Undergraduate Research Journal* and *SPIE Space + Defense*.
- **[amuse-cli](https://github.com/luccadimario/amuse-cli)** is a terminal
  now-playing display and live audio visualizer for Amazon Music, driving
  OS-native media APIs on Windows and Linux.

**Mostly working in** C/C++, Python, Go, TypeScript, Swift, PyTorch, ROS2.
